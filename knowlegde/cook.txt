====================================================
1. COOK
====================================================

A cook prepares meals according to the customer's requirements.

Available Types
- Part-Time Cook
- Full-Time Cook
- Live-In Cook

Possible Responsibilities
- Breakfast preparation
- Lunch preparation
- Dinner preparation
- Snacks preparation
- Vegetarian cooking
- Non-Vegetarian cooking
- North Indian cuisine
- South Indian cuisine
- Chinese cuisine
- Continental cuisine
- Kitchen cleaning after cooking

Information Required from Customer
- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- Address
- City
- State
- Pincode
- Start Date
- Preferred Timing
- Monthly Budget
- Cuisine Preference
- Meals Per Day
- Additional Notes
