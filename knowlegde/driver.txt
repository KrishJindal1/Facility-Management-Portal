====================================================
2. DRIVER
====================================================

A driver provides transportation services for individuals or families.

Available Types
- Personal Driver
- Full-Time Driver
- Part-Time Driver
- Temporary Driver

Possible Responsibilities
- Daily office travel
- School pickup and drop
- Airport pickup and drop
- Outstation travel
- Local city travel
- Vehicle maintenance assistance

Information Required from Customer
- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- Address
- City
- State
- Pincode
- Start Date
- Preferred Timing
- Monthly Budget
- Vehicle Type
- Driving Experience Required
- Additional Notes
